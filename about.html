Who we are

Advocacy, partnership, and communications — and the judgment to know which the moment calls for.

A US-based global consultancy built on a conviction shaped by every campaign, coalition, and cause we have touched.

That conviction: communications, advocacy, and partnership — principled, strategic, and deeply human — are the most powerful forces available to companies and organizations that want to bring about change for people.

Each is a genuine strength on its own, and we know when to pursue them separately and when to pursue them together.

When a company cannot move a market, when a policy stands between a patient and the treatment they need, when a clinical trial cannot find the community it is meant to serve, when a cause finds itself without the voice or the allies to carry it forward, these are often the levers to pull.

We are structured as a networked advisory practice, built to assemble the right people, voices, and strategies around any challenge. Time and again we have seen that organizations unable to achieve what they want — therapies reaching fewer patients than they should, coalitions whose voices go unheard, causes that stall at the edge of possible — are missing a better-coordinated advocacy and communications strategy rather than a better product.

So we ask not just what a client needs, but who else needs to be at the table, what others need to hear from them, and how to make sure the right people are listening.

01 / Mission

To advance patient outcomes and social progress by building the communications and advocacy infrastructure that transforms human insight into intelligent action.

Access to knowledge, treatment, care, and the systems that govern them is imperative. Advocacy, partnerships, campaigns, coalitions, and strategies are how we impact them.

03 / Mindset

Our partnership mindset

Advocacy does not happen alone. It happens when the right voices are raised, when shared purpose becomes shared strategy, and when individual commitment scales into collective power.

Every partnership we engage in — whether with a global advocacy organization or a single patient advocate — reflects genuine respect for that partner’s expertise, experience, and humanity. Partnerships are designed to create value for all parties. Trust is built over time, through consistent follow-through, honest communication, and shared commitment to mission.

Partnership is a sustained practice. This approach is why so many EmpathIQ relationships last for years.

04 / Leadership

Lee Lynch

CEO and Founding Partner

Lee has a passion for turning collaboration into real-world change, amplifying human insight through responsible technology to advance outcomes and causes. In her 25+ year career, she has led advocacy and alliance development functions and advocacy expert advisory boards at numerous leading communications consulting firms, and has helped build and manage many initiatives, campaigns, and organizations, with deep expertise in writing, strategy, and execution across advocacy and communications.

She founded EmpathIQ Advisors, formerly Lynch Advocacy Solutions, on the conviction that advocacy and communications done early and well are the most powerful forces available to organizations trying to change something for people. One of the things Lee loves to do most is to bring together an array of stakeholders to see the full picture and progress client goals and health and social impact causes through partnership-focused initiatives, coalitions, policy communications and public affairs, access-focused and strategic communications campaigns, organizational development, and stakeholder-focused research.

Lee is a trained professional moderator and an executive board member of the Culmore Clinic, a nonprofit offering free, compassionate medical care, counseling, and specialty referrals to uninsured adults in northern Virginia. Her support for neurodiverse individuals and causes led her to found Rocket Around, a content provider supporting adventure and life for neurodiverse families through its website, social channels, and books including Rocket Around Washington DC, Rocket Around Switzerland, and Rocket Around Germany. Lee is also a travel writer, publishing regularly for the Healthy Travel blog. Lee and her family live in Alexandria, VA.
