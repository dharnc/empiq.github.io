Who we are

Advocacy, partnership, and communications — and the judgment to know which the moment calls for.

A US-based global consultancy built on a conviction shaped by every campaign, coalition, and cause we have touched.

That conviction: advocacy — principled, strategic, and deeply human — is the most powerful force available to companies and organizations that want to bring about change for patients and people.

Three disciplines sit at the center of the work: advocacy, partnership, and strategic communications. Each is a genuine strength on its own, and we know when to pursue them separately and when to pursue them together.

When a company cannot move a market, when a policy stands between a patient and the treatment they need, when a clinical trial cannot find the community it is meant to serve, when a cause finds itself without the voice or the allies to carry it forward, advocacy is the lever.

We are structured as a networked advisory practice, built to assemble the right people, voices, and strategies around any challenge. Time and again we have seen that organizations unable to achieve what they want — therapies reaching fewer patients than they should, coalitions whose voices go unheard, causes that stall at the edge of possible — are missing a better-coordinated advocacy strategy rather than a better product.

So we ask not just what a client needs, but who else needs to be at the table, what others need to hear from them, and how to make sure the right people are listening.

Advocacy and alliance-building are rarely one-time events. They are ongoing disciplines, which is why a substantial share of our client relationships evolve into sustained, retained partnerships. We are built, staffed, and resourced to be that ongoing partner.

01 / Mission

To advance patient outcomes and social progress by building the advocacy infrastructure that transforms human insight into intelligent action.

The partnerships, campaigns, coalitions, and strategies. Access to knowledge, treatment, care, and the systems that govern them is imperative. Advocacy is how we secure it.

03 / Mindset

Our partnership mindset

Advocacy does not happen alone. It happens when the right voices find one another, when shared purpose becomes shared strategy, and when individual commitment scales into collective power.

Every partnership we engage in — whether with a global advocacy organization or a single patient advocate — reflects genuine respect for that partner’s expertise, experience, and humanity. Partnerships are designed to create value for all parties. Trust is built over time, through consistent follow-through, honest communication, and shared commitment to mission.

This is also why so many EmpathIQ relationships last for years. Partnership is not a one-time achievement. It is a sustained practice, and we are built to be the sustained partner for it.

04 / Leadership

Lee Lynch

CEO and Founding Partner

Lee has a passion for turning collaboration into real-world change, amplifying human insight through responsible technology to advance outcomes and causes. She has led advocacy and alliance development functions and advocacy expert advisory boards at numerous consulting firms, and has helped build and manage many initiatives, campaigns, and organizations, with deep expertise in writing, strategy, and execution across advocacy and communications.

She founded EmpathIQ Advisors, formerly Lynch Advocacy Solutions, on the conviction that advocacy done early and done well is the most powerful force available to organizations trying to change something for patients and people.

Her support for neurodiverse individuals and causes led her to found Rocket Around, a content provider supporting adventure and life for neurodiverse families through its website, social channels, and books including Rocket Around Washington DC, Rocket Around Switzerland, and Rocket Around Germany. Lee is a travel writer, a trained professional moderator, and an executive board member of the Culmore Clinic, a nonprofit offering free, compassionate medical care, counseling, and specialty referrals to uninsured adults in northern Virginia. Lee and her family live in Alexandria, VA.
